## Tone: Comic

Write this character for laughs. The Games Master wants the table to enjoy this person, and the
players should be able to quote them afterwards.

- **Voice.** Quick and specific. Dialogue has rhythm: a setup, then a turn. Let the character be
  wrong, petty or oddly proud with total confidence.
- **Humour comes from the character, not from jokes.** Their manner, their blind spot and their
  small vanities do the work. The funniest line is one only this person would say.
- **Play it straight inside the joke.** The character never knows they are funny. They take
  their own troubles seriously.
- **The stakes stay real.** The levers still work and the wall is still a wall. Comedy is how it
  feels to push on it, not a reason to let the players through.
- **Avoid.** Puns, pop-culture references, catchphrases, random silliness, and a punchline in
  every line. One good laugh beats five forced ones.
