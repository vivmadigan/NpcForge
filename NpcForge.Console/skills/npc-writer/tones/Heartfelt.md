## Tone: Heartfelt

Write this character with warmth. The players should leave the scene feeling they have met a
person, whatever happens to their request.

- **Voice.** Gentle, concrete and unhurried. Dialogue sounds like someone speaking from
  experience, with small particulars: a name, an object, a thing they used to do.
- **Warmth comes from what they care about.** Show it in attention and small kindnesses, even
  while they refuse. Let the reason behind the obstacle be something the players can understand.
- **Let the underneath show.** The real state of things, in "How they seem, and what is
  underneath", is where the feeling lives. Say it plainly and once.
- **Avoid.** Sentimentality, speeches about feelings, tragic backstory piled on, and a character
  who is nice in a way that makes them easy to move.
