1. **Who they are**
**Mara Venn**, innkeeper of the Brass Lantern, has spent twenty years learning which city secrets are worth hearing and which are worth surviving.

2. **Where they stand**
Mara knows the name of the fence the players seek, and knows exactly why they want it. They will not give it: the fence's business is not Mara's problem, and Mara wants **to be left alone**. "You've mistaken a public house for a public ledger."

3. **How they seem, and what is underneath**
They are brisk, welcoming, and relentlessly **nosy**, tilting their head whenever anyone offers even a stray detail. "Oh? You came in from the river ward? At this hour?"
Underneath, Mara is trying very hard to look unconnected. They believe they are **an excellent liar**, so the careful pauses, over-casual denials, and sudden interest in polishing a clean glass do not register to them as tells.

4. **Levers**
- **Weakness: easily impressed by confident people.** A calm, certain visitor who speaks as though Mara is plainly sensible and already understands the stakes can make them bend. "Well. Since you seem to have a proper grasp of how things are. I might tell you where to start looking."
