first tone files, all six used the same structure, levers and most of the same lines. Comic showed

offered seat or a mention of stew. The stronger files tell the tone to change every section of the
character and to be named from the first two lines. With them the owner guessed A and B Comic, C, D
and F Heartfelt and E Serious; the key had C as Serious. Comic was called "kinda weird and blunt" in
one take; left as is (lesson 010).
