## What it showed

- Roll again works against a real model: two rolls in the recording and three in the bakery run,
  each with new dice and the same answers, each named and written, and the question asked after
  each. Only the last character shown was saved.
- Under `--seed 1` the second character's dice are the ones the stand-in test predicted, so a roll
  again takes the next dice from the server's one `Random`.
- The list and the load find the saved character by number and name.
- *Needs the owner's eyes:* the three bakers, and whether the third is worth keeping.
